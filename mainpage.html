PLANETS
☀️ Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
🌎 It’s the smallest planet in our solar system.
🏃‍♂️ It moves around the Sun very fast – just 88 days!
🌡️ It’s super hot in the day and very cold at night.
🌕 Mercury has lots of holes on it called craters.
🌌 It doesn’t have any moons or rings.
👀 You can only see Mercury at sunrise or sunset.
🧲 It has a small magnetic field like Earth.

🔥 Venus is the hottest planet – hotter than Mercury!
🟡 It's the second planet from the Sun.
☁️ Venus has thick clouds that trap heat.
🔁 One day on Venus is longer than its year!
🔄 It spins the opposite way compared to most planets.
🌕 Venus is almost the same size as Earth.
🌟 It's super bright and can be seen at sunrise or sunset.
🚫 Venus has no moons or rings.

🌍 Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
🌎 It’s the only planet known to have life.
🌱 Earth has plants, animals, and people living on it.
🌧️ Earth has weather like rain, snow, and wind.
🌞 Earth orbits the Sun in about 365 days.
🌑 Earth has one moon that orbits it.
🌊 Most of Earth is covered by water – oceans, lakes, and rivers.
⛅ Earth has different seasons because of its tilted axis.

🔴 Mars is called the "Red Planet" because of its reddish surface.
🌑 Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.
🪐 Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.
💨 Mars has a thin atmosphere, so it’s very cold there.
🌋 Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.
🌒 A day on Mars is just a little longer than a day on Earth.
💧 Scientists think there may have been water on Mars in the past.
🚀 Mars is a target for space missions to learn more about the planet.

PLANETS
💫 Saturn is the second-largest planet in our solar system.
💍 Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings, which are made of ice and rock.
🌑 Saturn has 83 known moons, with Titan being the largest.
🌞 A year on Saturn is about 29 Earth years, but a day is just 10.7 hours long.
🌬️ Saturn is made mostly of gas, so it doesn’t have a solid surface.
🔄 Saturn’s rings are constantly changing and moving due to its strong gravity.
🛸 Saturn has been visited by many space missions, including NASA’s Cassini spacecraft.
🧊 Saturn’s rings are the most complex and largest in the entire solar system.

PLANETS
🌍 Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun.
🛸 Uranus is a gas giant, which means it’s made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
🌑 Uranus has 27 known moons, with Titania being the largest.
🔄 Uranus spins on its side, which makes it very unique compared to other planets.
🌞 A day on Uranus lasts only 17 hours and 14 minutes.
🌕 Uranus has a faint system of rings, but they are not as bright as Saturn’s.
❄️ Uranus is very cold, with temperatures reaching -224°C (-371°F).
🚀 Uranus has been visited only by NASA's Voyager 2 spacecraft.

PLANETS
🌌 Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun.
🌑 Neptune is a gas giant, made mostly of hydrogen, helium, and water.
🌬️ Neptune has the strongest winds in the solar system – they can reach speeds of 2,100 km/h (1,300 mph)!
🌑 Neptune has 14 known moons, with Triton being the largest.
🔵 Neptune is known for its beautiful blue color, caused by methane in its atmosphere.
❄️ Neptune is extremely cold, with temperatures around -214°C (-353°F).
🌀 Neptune has a big storm called the Great Dark Spot, similar to Jupiter’s Great Red Spot.
🚀 Neptune has only been visited by NASA’s Voyager 2 spacecraft, which flew by it in 1989.
